## Direction contract

THESIS: A sellable, white-label B2B operations console reads as a calm instrument the operator trusts for hours at a time, not a generated "AI app" — it refuses the purple-gradient/glow/glass default and the single-business brand skin.

OWN-WORLD: Warm light ground (#F5F6F3) with white surface cards, bordered not shadowed; a dark graphite-green fixed sidebar (#202925) with a muted deep-green accent (#3F6F62) reserved for primary actions, the active-nav indicator, and key metrics only; compact amber/blue/brown semantic tones (warning/info/pending) for status badges, never saturated; Inter-less system sans (Armenian has no Latin-display glyphs to spare); 8–10px radii, no pill-everything; icons at 1.75–2px stroke (Lucide).

STORY: An operator scans the sidebar to find their section in one glance, reads KPI tiles and tables without hunting for meaning in color, and edits records through the same input/button/table language on every screen — including the eleven CRM modules not built yet, shown as quiet "Շուտով" placeholders so the product's intended shape is legible without faking functionality.

FIRST VIEWPOINT: Fixed 256px dark sidebar (brand mark + grouped nav) | compact 64px top bar (breadcrumbs, disabled search/notifications, user menu) | content at 24–32px padding opening with a page header (title + primary action) directly above the KPI/table content — no hero, no empty space tax.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (exact hex palette, radii, control heights, component rules, and IA dictated by the user) — concept-seed roll was skipped per new-work.md's "pinned beats the roll" rule. seed: none (pinned brief).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
